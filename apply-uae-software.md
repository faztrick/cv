# Apply Guide — Software Engineer roles (UAE)

This playbook helps you apply fast and track outcomes for Software Engineer roles in Dubai/Abu Dhabi.

---

## 1) Prepare assets

- Export to PDF:
  - `resumes/variants/resume-uae.md` → Resume (UAE)
  - `cover-letters/cover-letter-uae-software.md` → Cover letter
- Email body: `emails/uae-generic-software.txt`
- Links helper: `docs/send-mail-links.md` → Generic Gmail compose link
- Portfolio: <https://uaecodes.com>, GitHub: <https://github.com/faztrick>, LinkedIn: <https://linkedin.com/in/faztrick>

Tip: Keep a `docs/` folder (local) with the latest PDFs to drag‑and‑drop.

---

## 2) Where to apply

- LinkedIn Jobs: filter by “Dubai, United Arab Emirates” or “Abu Dhabi, United Arab Emirates”
- Bayt, NaukriGulf, GulfTalent, Indeed UAE
- Company career pages (bookmark target companies)

Filters to use:

- Titles: Software Engineer, Senior Software Engineer, Backend Engineer, Full‑Stack Engineer
- Experience: Senior/Lead or Mid‑Senior
- Tech: Node.js/TypeScript, Flutter, Python, .NET, Docker
- Posted: Last 24 hours / Last 7 days

---

## 3) Quick-apply steps

- For LinkedIn Easy Apply: upload resume PDF, include short note referencing your cover letter (paste from email body if note field allows).
- For portals: attach resume PDF; where cover letters are optional, paste the first 1–2 paragraphs from `cover-letter-uae-software.md`.
- For direct email postings:
  1. Open the generic Gmail link in `docs/send-mail-links.md`.
  2. Paste body from `emails/uae-generic-software.txt`.
  3. Attach PDFs (resume + cover letter) and send.

---

## 4) Tracking and follow‑ups

- Log every application in `job-tracker-uae.csv` with: date, source, company, role, link, status, next follow‑up date.
- Follow‑up cadence:
  - T+3 business days: polite check‑in, share portfolio again.
  - T+10 business days: second follow‑up; ask if a better matching role exists.
- Reach out to hiring managers/recruiters on LinkedIn with a 2–3 sentence note + resume link.

---

## 5) ATS notes (UAE)

- Keep file names clean: `Muhammed-FasilPV-Resume-UAE-Software.pdf`.
- Keywords to include in resume/profile (already present but emphasize in job descriptions where relevant):
  - Node.js, TypeScript, Express, REST APIs, Microservices, Docker, CI/CD, Nginx, PM2
  - Flutter, MVVM, Offline‑first, Hive, Firebase
  - MySQL, MongoDB, Indexing, Caching, Replication
  - AI/Automation: WhatsApp Business API, Telegram Bot, YOLO, Whisper
  - Networking: MikroTik, WireGuard, VPN, NAT, DHCP
- Keep responsibilities/results in bullet points with verbs + outcomes (latency reduction, uptime, cost, SLA).

---

## 6) Ready-to-send snippets

- Subject: “Application – Software Engineer (Backend/Full‑Stack) – Muhammed Fasil PV”
- First line hook (optional for portals): “Dubai‑based engineer with 13+ years shipping production systems (ERP/POS/QMS/IoT) using Node.js/TypeScript, Flutter, and Docker. Immediately available (Company Visa).”

---

## 7) Daily routine (30–45 min)

- 10 min: scan and shortlist fresh roles across portals
- 15 min: apply to 3–5 roles using the prepared assets
- 5 min: log in tracker, schedule follow‑ups
- 5–15 min: outreach to 1–2 hiring managers on LinkedIn
