## 💡 Pro Tips

### Email Customization

- Always research the company before applying
- Mention specific projects/technologies they use
- Align your experience with their job requirements
- Keep it concise (under 300 words)

### LinkedIn Strategy

- Set profile to "Open to Work" (visible to recruiters only)
- Add keywords: AI, ML, IoT, Flutter, Node.js, Enterprise Systems
- Post 2-3 times per week about your projects
- Comment on tech posts from target companies

### Application Timing

- Best days: Tuesday-Thursday
- Best time: 8-10 AM (when recruiters start work)
- Avoid: Friday afternoons, weekends

### Follow-up Timing

- First follow-up: 5-7 days after application
- Second follow-up: 10-14 days after first
- Don't follow up more than twice

## 🆘 Need Help?

### Common Issues

**Q: No responses after 2 weeks?**

- A: Increase application volume to 5-7/day
- Expand search to more platforms
- Try direct company outreach
- Get LinkedIn profile reviewed

**Q: How to handle rejection?**

- A: Update tracker status
- Request feedback if possible
- Apply to similar roles at other companies
- Keep momentum going

**Q: Should I mention salary expectations?**

- A: Not in initial email
- Wait for them to ask
- When asked: "I'm targeting 15-18k AED based on my experience, but I'm flexible based on the complete package"
