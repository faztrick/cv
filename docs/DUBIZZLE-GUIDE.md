# Dubizzle Job Search Guide

Dubizzle is a powerful tool in Dubai, but it works differently from LinkedIn or Indeed. It is a classifieds site, meaning direct contact is key.

## 1. Optimizing Your Profile

* **Upload the New CV:** Ensure `resume-fasil-software-2025.pdf` is on your profile.
* **Phone Number:** Make sure your WhatsApp number is visible and verified. Recruiters on Dubizzle often WhatsApp directly.

## 2. "Job Wanted" Ads (Highly Recommended)

Many recruiters search the "Job Seekers" section instead of posting jobs.

* **Post a Free Ad:** Go to "Place an Ad" -> "Jobs" -> "Job Seekers" -> "Software Development".
* **Title:** "Senior Full Stack Engineer (React/Node.js) - 13+ Years Exp - Available Immediately"
* **Description:**
    > Senior Software Engineer with 13+ years of experience in Dubai and India.
    > Expert in:
    > * React.js & Node.js (Full Stack)
    > * Mobile App Development (Flutter)
    > * AI Integration (OpenAI, Python)
    >
    > Architected Enterprise ERP systems for 50+ branches.
    > Available immediately. Valid UAE Visa (if applicable) or Visit Visa.
    >
    > Contact: +971 555923545 (Call/WhatsApp)
* **Photos:** Upload a professional photo of yourself and 2-3 screenshots of your best projects (IdolMEA, Mobile Apps).

## 3. Searching & Applying

* **Search Keywords:** "Software Developer", "React", "Flutter", "IT Manager".
* **Filter:** Select "Direct from Employer" to avoid agencies if possible.
* **Applying:**
  * **Don't just click Apply.**
  * **Look for a Phone Number:** If listed, send a WhatsApp message immediately.
  * **WhatsApp Template:**
        > "Hi, I saw your ad for the Software Engineer role on Dubizzle. I am a Senior Full Stack Dev (13 yrs exp) in Dubai, expert in React & Node.js. Here is my portfolio: uaecodes.com. Available to join immediately."

## 4. Safety Tips

* **Avoid Scams:** Never pay money for a job application.
* **Verify Company:** If the company name is hidden, ask for it before sending sensitive documents.
